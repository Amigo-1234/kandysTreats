About Kandy’s Treats

Since its inauguration on 1st October 2023, Kandy’s Treats has gained fame as one of the best Nigerian food restaurants in the Ekiti State University, Iworoko / Ado-Ekiti area — serving mouthwatering fresh flavors and a great range of dishes characterized by a high-quality, rich Nigerian flare.

From smoky jollof to chilled drinks and quick bites, we serve familiar Nigerian flavours with a modern glow.
